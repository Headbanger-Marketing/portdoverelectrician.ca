Electrical decisions belong early in the renovation

A renovation changes how the space will be used and what equipment it must serve. Provide the drawings and appliance specifications before the electrical scope is finalized. A Licensed Electrical Contractor can assess retained wiring, new circuits and the proposed loads. Municipal building permissions and ESA electrical notification are separate matters, and the project may require both.

Prepare the scope for your Port Dover property

List the equipment the new bathroom will use, such as a fan, mirror lighting or a heated floor, and gather the product sheets. Photograph the existing fixtures and note where the nearest panel sits.

Ask the contractor whether a building permit and an ESA notification are both needed for this renovation, who files each, and what documents will remain with you for a lender or insurer.

What should the assessment establish?

One bathroom of a Port Dover home is due for renovation. How the room will be used shapes which new circuits it needs, and the existing wiring is judged piece by piece as staying or being replaced. Where new cable can run is worked out, including through any finished surface. If the bathroom sits above a finished basement, the contractor names the finishes that will be opened. The scope lists the equipment each circuit serves and the notification the work needs. A lender or insurer may ask how the renovation changed the wiring, so keep the scope with your drawings.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Do I need drawings before the bathroom wiring is scoped?

Drawings help, because the contractor can then match circuits to the fan, lighting and any heated floor you chose. Even a hand sketch with the fixtures marked gives a lender or insurer a clearer record.

Does a lender care who did the wiring in a renovated bathroom?

It may, and only the lender can confirm what it needs. Keep the contractor's business name, the ESA notification record and the written scope together, and check the licence through ESA before work starts.

Useful project details

  • Drawings and intended use of each space
  • Appliance and equipment model information
  • Construction schedule and other trades
  • Existing installation records if available
  • Person authorized to approve changes

Only photograph labels and locations that you can see safely. Do not remove electrical covers or approach damaged equipment to gather information.
